I wasn't super satisfied with the list I came up with in the previous blog post for the remaining work needed on Drone Drifters. I still think the things I laid out in that post are relevant and I believe that it was important to get those thoughts out of my head and onto paper, but I'm hoping to break down this large amorphous burden of work that forms when I think about Drone Drifters into a bunch of smaller, more manageable chunks.

To that end, in this post I'm just gonna try to craft a version one of a Table of Contents for the game, to act as both a list of the content that the game requires, and a first pass at how that content should be organized.
